Need a window cleaner in Wandsworth?

These answers cover the details that most often affect a residential or commercial Window Cleaning request.

Can you clean windows above extensions or conservatories?

You can include them in the request, but access must be reviewed before the final quote is confirmed. Describe any extension roof, conservatory or obstacle beneath the windows.

Do you offer regular Window Cleaning in Wandsworth?

Yes. You can request one-off cleaning or a regular 4-, 8- or 12-week pattern where the property and access are suitable.

Do you cover houses, flats and commercial properties?

Yes. Namdar accepts requests for houses, flats and commercial properties within the live service area, subject to the postcode and access review.

What happens after I receive the online estimate?

The online amount is a guide estimate. Namdar reviews the request, sends the final quote, and only after you accept it can you choose an available appointment.
