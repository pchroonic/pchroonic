Can you clean windows behind balconies or on upper floors?

You can request them, but safe exterior access must be reviewed. Add the floor level, balcony layout and any obstacles when you submit the quote details.

Can roof lights and glass doors be included?

Yes. Roof lights, exterior glass doors and other panes can be added to the request so Namdar can include them in the review.

Can I arrange regular Window Cleaning in Greenwich?

You can request a one-off clean or a regular 4-, 8- or 12-week pattern, subject to the property, access and current service availability.
