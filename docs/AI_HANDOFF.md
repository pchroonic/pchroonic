# Flexible Payment & Deposit Policy Engine — RELEASE CANDIDATE

Branch: `feature/flexible-payment-policy-engine-20260915`
Version: `6.4.35-payment-policy-engine-1`
Base main: `64de3f653d294a31afb4ef690189017272c33766`.

## Owner-approved design intent
The owner asked for a system that can learn and change over time rather than a permanent fixed deposit. The intended architecture is:
- Admin can change deposit policy for future bookings whenever business experience supports it;
- larger final quote values can require larger deposit percentages/minimums;
- each customer booking freezes the payment policy presented at appointment request time;
- later policy edits cannot silently increase an existing booking’s deposit;
- remaining balance can be due at completion or a configured number of hours after the job;
- overdue balances escalate through reminders, future-booking hold and recovery review;
- no automatically increasing or compounding consumer penalty is generated by this engine;
- B2B statutory interest/recovery is kept separate and manual until business-customer classification and legal/commercial controls are deliberately built.

## `lib/payment-policy.js`
Extended payment policy fields:
- `revision`
- `active`
- `mode`: optional / deposit_required / full_required
- `depositStrategy`: flat / tiered
- flat `depositPercent`, `minimumDeposit`
- `depositBands[]`: minAmount, maxAmount, percent, minimumDeposit
- `allowFullPayment`
- `balanceDueHours` (0–168)
- overdue reminder days
- overdue booking-hold day
- overdue final-review day
- consumer monetary late fees forced false
- commercial recovery mode forced manual_review
- existing headline allowance fields unchanged.

Helpers:
- `validateDepositBands` enforces £0 start, contiguous bands, open-ended final band, and non-decreasing protection.
- `resolveDepositRule` selects the rule by final job value.
- `snapshotPaymentPolicy` freezes exact customer-facing rules and £ deposit.
- `paymentPolicyFromSnapshot` restores contract rules without inheriting later deposit changes.
- `paymentRequirementMet` checks actual net money received against locked deposit/full amount.
- `checkoutPlan(...forceBalance)` makes completed/due invoices request the full outstanding balance.
- `balanceDueAt`, `overdueStage` provide due/hold/recovery calculation.
- `commercialLatePaymentPreview` models 8% + supplied Bank base rate and £40/£70/£100 recovery bands, but always returns `automatic:false`, `reviewRequired:true`.

The code fallback remains inactive/optional 20% £10 only because a safe default is needed. There is currently no production payments settings row and these values are not commercially approved.

## Admin policy editor
`admin-payment-settings.js` + `api/admin-payment-settings.js` now support:
- flat or value-tiered deposits;
- dynamic add/remove band editor;
- balance-due timing;
- reminder days;
- booking hold day;
- recovery review day;
- policy revision display;
- existing full-pay option and headline price allowance.

Every Admin save increments `policy_revision` and is audit logged. Invalid tier bands cannot be activated. Stripe activation is still blocked unless provider secret + verified webhook are configured. Saving an inactive policy is allowed so the owner can prepare settings without enabling customer payment.

## Customer booking commitment
`api/customer-quote-action.js` adds a safe `paymentCommitment` to the signed-in quote scheduling response.

`account-booking-policy.js` (v6.4.35):
- shows the exact deposit/full/optional commitment before appointment request;
- shows balance due timing and overdue escalation;
- states no automatic consumer penalty/compounding fee/interest;
- tracks `paymentPolicyRevision` and blocks submit until terms load;
- includes the presented revision in `/api/booking`.

`api/booking-core.js`:
- loads current policy server-side;
- rejects stale/missing payment revision;
- if payment is active+required but provider is unavailable, blocks the booking request rather than taking a commitment that cannot be paid;
- checks for materially overdue earlier active-policy invoices whose recorded hold threshold is reached;
- freezes `payment_policy_revision`, `payment_policy_locked_at`, `payment_policy_snapshot`, `deposit_required` on the booking;
- customer/staff booking emails record the revision and customer payment terms.

## Invoice and checkout enforcement
`lib/server.js` overrides `ensureInvoiceForBooking` and copies an active booking snapshot to invoices. It records deposit/due/hold/review settings and, only for active-policy snapshots, changes invoice `due_at` to scheduled job end + configured `balanceDueHours`.

Legacy or payment-off bookings keep the old invoice due behavior (existing base implementation is job end + 7 days).

`api/create-checkout.js`:
- still requires live Stripe readiness and global payment activation;
- restores frozen contract policy from booking/invoice snapshot;
- legacy bookings are not retroactively forced into a new deposit;
- completed or due invoices use full outstanding balance;
- idempotent Stripe checkout remains unchanged.

`api/admin-booking-update.js`:
- confirmation of a customer booking uses the booking’s frozen policy;
- actual net paid must meet the locked deposit/full requirement;
- a tiny part payment does not satisfy a larger required deposit;
- manually recorded legitimate payment can satisfy the frozen contract even if online Stripe is temporarily unavailable later.

## Billing / overdue behavior
`api/customer-billing.js` + `account-payments.js` expose only customer-safe fields:
- payment revision;
- frozen deposit amount;
- balance due timing;
- overdue days;
- booking-hold/recovery-review state;
- explicit `consumerMonetaryLateFees:false`.
No provider processor-cost/private Stripe fields are exposed.

`lib/business-followup-batched.js`:
- active-policy invoices use the reminder days frozen in their payment snapshot;
- legacy/inactive-policy invoices keep existing 1/8/15/29-day reminders;
- reminder metadata records hold/review thresholds but never mutates invoice money.

A new customer appointment can be paused when a previous invoice with its own active payment policy crosses its own recorded hold threshold.
