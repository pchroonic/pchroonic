Before you request a quote

Common questions, answered clearly.

The estimate starts the conversation. Nothing is booked until the final quote is accepted and an appointment is submitted.

Is the instant price the final price?

No. It is a guide estimate based on the details you enter. Namdar reviews the job before sending the final quote.

Do I need to pay to get an estimate?

No. The estimate and final-quote review do not require payment. Online payment remains separate from the quote process.

Can I upload photos?

Yes. Signed-in customers can attach private job photos to help Namdar understand access, condition and the work required.

How do I choose an appointment?

Once you accept the final quote in My Namdar, the account shows currently available appointment windows to request.

Can I change a booking later?

For eligible future appointments, My Namdar lets you request another available slot or request cancellation. The original booking stays in place until Namdar reviews the request.

How do I know if Namdar covers my address?

Enter the exact UK postcode. The site verifies the postcode and checks current Namdar service coverage before booking.
