What is included

A clearer way to arrange window cleaning.

Namdar prices window cleaning using the property details you provide, including window count, storeys, access and any extra complexity. The website gives a guide estimate first; Namdar then reviews the request and sends the final quote to My Namdar for acceptance.

  • Exterior glass, frames and sills
  • Homes, flats and commercial properties
  • One-off or regular service requests
  • Final quote reviewed by Namdar before booking
