Common questions

Window Cleaning FAQs

Can I get a price online?

Yes. The website gives a guide estimate from the property and job details you provide. Namdar reviews the request before sending the final price.

Are frames and sills included?

Exterior frames and exterior sills are included in the Window Cleaning request unless the final quote says otherwise for a particular property or access situation.

Can I request regular Window Cleaning?

Yes. You can request 4-, 8- or 12-week cleaning. Namdar confirms the regular price and schedule before activation.

What if this is the first clean or the windows are very dirty?

Select the condition that best describes the property and add photos if useful. Namdar reviews first-clean or heavy-build-up requirements before confirming the final price.

Do I pay when I request the estimate?

No. The estimate stage does not take payment. The final quote is reviewed first and any booking/payment step happens separately.
